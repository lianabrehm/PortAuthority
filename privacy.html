1. Scope of This Privacy Notice

Port Authority is a proposed logistics and shipping platform supporting businesses with international cargo transportation, shipment management, shipping supplies, and cargo security.

This Privacy Notice describes how personal information may be collected, used, disclosed, retained, and protected when individuals interact with the Port Authority website and its services.

It is intended to cover business customers, authorized account users, shipment senders and recipients, service partners, and website visitors.

2. Who Is Responsible for Your Data?

Port Authority is the proposed operator of this platform. For personal data processed under its own purposes and decisions, Port Authority would act as a data controller where applicable law uses that designation.

In some business arrangements, Port Authority may instead process information on behalf of another organization. The parties' responsibilities would be established through the relevant agreements.

Verified business contact details and any legally required representative or data protection officer information must be added before production launch.

3. Personal Information We May Collect

Identity and Business Information

  • Full name and job title.
  • Company name and business address.
  • Business account identifiers.
  • Authorized account representatives.

Contact Information

  • Email addresses.
  • Telephone numbers.
  • Billing and delivery addresses.

Account and Authentication Information

  • Usernames and account identifiers.
  • Securely stored password hashes.
  • Login and authentication records.
  • Account permissions and preferences.
  • Acceptance of applicable terms.

Shipment and Transaction Information

  • Shipment reference numbers.
  • Sender and recipient details.
  • Pickup and delivery locations.
  • Cargo descriptions and quantities.
  • Shipment status and delivery records.
  • Orders, invoices, and service requests.
  • Customs or trade documentation when required for a service.

Technical and Usage Information

  • IP addresses and browser information.
  • Device and operating system details.
  • Access timestamps and security logs.
  • Website interactions and preferences.
  • Cookie and session information.

Not all categories will necessarily be collected. The production notice must accurately reflect the implemented features.

4. How Information Is Collected

Personal information may be provided directly when users register accounts, submit forms, request shipping services, place orders, or contact support.

Information may also be generated automatically through server logs, security monitoring, cookies, and application activity.

For logistics services, shipment information may be received from business customers, authorized shipping partners, or transportation providers.

5. Purposes and Legal Bases

Port Authority intends to process personal information only for defined and appropriate business purposes.

Providing Logistics Services

Information may be used to register accounts, process orders, coordinate shipments, communicate delivery updates, and provide customer support.

Legal and Regulatory Obligations

Certain shipment information may be needed to meet applicable customs, trade, recordkeeping, or transportation requirements.

Platform Security

Technical information may be processed to authenticate users, investigate suspicious activity, prevent fraud, and protect information systems.

Communication and Marketing

Service-related communications may be necessary to fulfill customer requests. Optional marketing communications would be subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

Legal Bases Under Applicable GDPR Laws

Where the EU GDPR or UK GDPR applies, processing must rely on an appropriate legal basis. Depending on the activity, this may include performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests, or consent.

Each actual processing activity must be mapped to its correct legal basis before launch.

6. Disclosure of Personal Information

Information may need to be disclosed to authorized third parties for legitimate logistics and business purposes.

  • Shipping carriers and transportation providers.
  • Freight forwarders and customs brokers.
  • Warehouses and fulfillment service providers.
  • Hosting, database, and technical service providers.
  • Payment processors, if real payments are introduced.
  • Government or customs authorities where required by applicable law.

Port Authority should limit disclosures to information appropriate for the purpose and establish suitable contractual safeguards with service providers.

Any sale or sharing of personal information for advertising purposes must be separately evaluated and disclosed where required by law.

7. International Data Transfers

Port Authority is designed to support international shipping and may involve business relationships across multiple countries.

International shipping does not necessarily mean personal data is transferred internationally. Any actual transfers must be identified and assessed.

Where applicable GDPR rules restrict transfers outside the relevant jurisdiction, Port Authority would need an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards.

8. Information Security

Port Authority's proposed cybersecurity program is intended to reduce risks of unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure of information.

Planned safeguards include:

  • HTTPS/TLS for website communications.
  • Secure password hashing and account authentication.
  • Role-based access controls and least privilege.
  • Database access restrictions.
  • System and vulnerability monitoring.
  • Security patching and configuration management.
  • Database backups and recovery testing.
  • Incident response and breach notification procedures.

These are intended controls, not representations that all protections have already been deployed.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for its stated purpose, applicable legal requirements, and legitimate security or operational needs.

Different retention periods may apply to account records, shipment documentation, transaction histories, security logs, and backups.

Before production launch, Port Authority must establish a retention schedule specifying periods or objective criteria for each category, together with secure deletion procedures.

10. Individual Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal data.

  • Access to personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion in applicable circumstances.
  • Restriction of certain processing.
  • Objection to certain processing.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complaint to an appropriate supervisory authority.

Requests may be subject to identity verification, legal exceptions, and applicable response deadlines.

A functional request submission and response process must be established before offering these services.

11. California Privacy Disclosures

If Port Authority qualifies as a business subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, it must provide the notices and rights required by that law.

Applicable rights may include knowing, accessing, correcting, and deleting personal information; opting out of certain sales or sharing; limiting certain uses of sensitive personal information; and protection against unlawful discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

A compliant California privacy policy may also need to disclose categories of information collected, sources, purposes, recipient categories, retention periods, and applicable sale or sharing practices, including required lookback disclosures.

Port Authority must determine whether the CCPA applies and complete these disclosures using verified practices.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use necessary cookies or session identifiers to support authentication, account security, and core website functions.

Optional analytics, personalization, or marketing technologies should be disclosed and controlled according to applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

Where required, users should be able to manage preferences and withdraw consent without disabling necessary services.

13. Chatbots and Automated Processing

Port Authority may provide an AI-assisted chatbot to help visitors navigate services or obtain general shipping information.

If implemented, users should be informed when interacting with an automated system and advised not to submit unnecessary sensitive personal information.

The team must document whether chatbot messages are stored, transmitted to an external AI provider, or used for model training.

Any automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects would require additional assessment and disclosures where applicable.

14. Children's Privacy

Port Authority is designed as a business-to-business logistics platform and is not intended for children.

The production platform should establish appropriate procedures if it becomes aware of personal information collected from children in circumstances requiring additional legal protections.

15. External Websites and Services

The Port Authority website may contain links to third-party shipping, transportation, or informational services.

Those third parties may maintain separate privacy notices and data practices. Users should review applicable third-party notices before submitting information to them.

16. Changes to This Notice

This notice may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, processing activities, business operations, or legal requirements.

The current version date should be displayed, and material changes should be communicated where required by applicable law.

17. Contact and Privacy Requests

Questions about this draft notice can be directed through the Port Authority contact page.

Before operational launch, Port Authority must publish verified contact details and establish any legally required privacy request channels and complaint procedures.
